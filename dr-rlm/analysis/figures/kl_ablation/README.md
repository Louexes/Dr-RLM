## `fig_kl_ablation.{pdf,png}`

Three vertically stacked panels sharing the step axis (1-95):
(a) median finalized-report length (chars), with the ~4,900-char SFT-init
    dotted reference line;
(b) R(finalized) -- mean report reward over finalized trees only;
(c) citations per child node.

Each panel shows the faint raw per-step series plus a centered rolling mean
(window=15), smoothed SEPARATELY within each phase -- the mean never
rolls across the splice, so the visible discontinuity at step 50
is real, not a smoothing artifact. A vertical dashed line at step 50
marks the coefficient change ("KL 0.01 -> 0.05"); a faint background tint
distinguishes the coef=0.01 region (steps 1-50) from the coef=0.05
region (steps 51-95).
